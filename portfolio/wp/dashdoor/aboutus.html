About DashDoor Real Estate

Your trusted partner in finding the perfect rental property across Canada

Our Story

Founded in 2015, DashDoor Real Estate began with a simple mission: to revolutionize how people find rental properties in Canada. What started as a small local operation in Toronto has grown into one of the country's most trusted rental platforms.

We recognized that the traditional rental process was often frustrating and time-consuming. Tenants struggled to find reliable listings, while property managers faced challenges connecting with qualified renters. DashDoor was created to bridge this gap.

Today, we're proud to serve thousands of satisfied tenants and property owners across 25 major Canadian cities. Our platform features over 5,000 verified properties, from cozy studio apartments to spacious family homes and commercial spaces.
