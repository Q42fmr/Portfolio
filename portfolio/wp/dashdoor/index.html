Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about renting with DashDoor

What fees should I expect when renting through DashDoor?

▼

Our application fee is $50 CAD, which covers background and credit checks. Security deposits typically range from half to one month's rent, depending on the property and your credit history. There are no hidden fees - we clearly outline all costs before you apply.

How long does the application process take?

▼

Most applications are processed within 24-48 hours. Applications submitted during business hours with all required documents typically receive faster processing. We'll notify you immediately once approval is granted.

Do I need to visit properties in person?

▼

Not necessarily! Many properties offer virtual tours, which you can take from the comfort of your home. However, in-person visits are recommended when possible, especially for properties in your area. We offer both options to accommodate all situations.

What happens if I have issues with my rental property after moving in?

▼

Our 24/7 support team is here to help with any issues. Whether it's maintenance requests, lease questions, or emergencies, contact us anytime. We work closely with property managers to ensure prompt resolution of all concerns.

Can I rent if I'm moving from outside Canada?

▼

Absolutely! We assist many international renters. You'll need valid identification, proof of income or employment offer, and possibly additional documentation depending on your specific situation. Our team can guide you through these requirements.

How do I know if a property is still available?

▼

All listings on our site are updated in real-time. Properties marked as "Available" are currently unoccupied and ready for immediate viewing. If a property receives an application, we update its status immediately to avoid disappointment.
